The Heart Behind the Work

A Passion for
Helping Children Communicate

Tonya Belczyk is a registered Speech-Language Pathologist with a Master of Science degree from the University of Pittsburgh. She holds registration with CASLPO (College of Audiologists and Speech-Language Pathologists of Ontario) and holds the Certificate of Clinical Competence (CCC-SLP) from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA), reflecting her commitment to the highest standards of clinical practice.

Based in Oakville, Burlington, and Milton, Ontario, Tonya offers individualized speech and language assessments and therapy services for children of all ages — from early intervention through school age and beyond. Her work spans in-home, in-clinic, and virtual settings, making quality care accessible to families across the community.

Tonya's therapeutic philosophy centres on meeting each child exactly where they are. By building genuine connections with the children and families she works with, and creating a warm, low-pressure environment, she fosters real progress — all while making the journey feel like play. Her clinical interests span a broad and diverse range of communication needs, with a particular passion for supporting neurodiverse learners and those with complex communication profiles.
