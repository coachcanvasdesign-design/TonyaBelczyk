The Heart Behind the Work

Hi, I'm Tonya — let's talk

Hi, I'm Tonya Belczyk, a Speech-Language Pathologist providing personalized, evidence-based in-home speech and language therapy for children throughout Oakville, Burlington, Milton, and surrounding communities.

With over 10 years of pediatric experience, I earned both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Speech-Language Pathology from the University of Pittsburgh. I am licensed to practice in Ontario and hold certification through the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA), allowing me to bring a broad, evidence-based perspective to every child and family I support.

I believe every child has the potential to communicate with confidence. My goal is to make therapy engaging, practical, and individualized, working closely with families to build meaningful communication skills that carry over into everyday life.

How We Work

Service Type

In Home

Speech and language therapy is provided in the comfort of your home throughout Oakville, Burlington, Milton, and surrounding communities. By working in a familiar environment, children often feel more relaxed, engaged, and ready to learn.

In-home therapy allows skills to be practiced within everyday routines, making it easier for children and families to carry strategies over into daily life. Services include comprehensive assessments, individualized intervention, parent coaching, consultation, and Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) support for children from birth to 18 years of age.

Service Type

Virtual

Virtual speech and language services are available to families across Ontario, providing convenient access to high-quality, evidence-based care from wherever you are.

Online sessions are interactive, engaging, and personalized to each child's communication goals. Whether targeting speech, language, literacy, social communication, or AAC, virtual therapy offers flexibility while keeping parents actively involved in the therapy process.
