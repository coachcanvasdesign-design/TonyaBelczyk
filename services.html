Clinical Services

Services

Every child's path is different. Here's how we guide families from the very first conversation to meaningful, lasting progress — with clarity and care at every step.

01

Step One

Assessment

Assessment involves the administration of assessment materials, followed by careful scoring and goal-setting to guide next steps. Tonya believes in spending just enough time on assessment to get intervention started — since ongoing assessment is built into every treatment plan, and families see the greatest value in actual therapy sessions.

Following the initial assessment, families receive a personalized intervention plan outlining clear treatment goals, so everyone understands the path forward.

02

Step Two

Intervention

Weekly intervention sessions provide focused, one-on-one therapy time tailored to each child's goals. In addition to direct sessions, families receive home programming — practical instructions and activity suggestions — to support progress between visits.

Clear, consistent communication is a priority throughout the process, ensuring families stay informed and involved every step of the way.

Practical Info

Policies & Logistics

Cancellations & No-Shows

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice when cancelling or rescheduling a session. Appointments cancelled with less notice or missed entirely will be charged at the full session rate. We understand life happens — please reach out as early as possible.

Insurance Coverage

We are a private-pay practice and do not direct bill insurance providers. Upon request, we issue detailed receipts (superbills) that you can submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement. Many extended health plans include SLP services — we recommend checking your coverage in advance.

Invoices & Payments

Invoices are issued after each session and are due within 7 days. We accept e-transfer and major credit cards. A card on file may be required to secure your booking. Please reach out if you have any questions about payment options.
